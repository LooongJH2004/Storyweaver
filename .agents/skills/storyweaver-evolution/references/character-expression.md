## Separate the causal layers

| Layer | Evidence and design question | Smallest intervention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Referent | Does a public action, dialogue, purpose or private thought attach a form of address to the correct visible person ref? | Attach an explicitly reviewed, source-backed expression reference locally to one CURRENT PEOPLE row. |
| Temporal dependency | Does speech presume an attempt succeeded before a delivered result? | Require speech to remain meaningful on failure, or wait for the actual delivered result; changing array order alone does not provide a result. |
| Past facts | Does a preference, example, fear or proposal become invented earlier experience? | Distinguish an established observation from a style cue, without blocking intentional lies or mistaken beliefs. |
| Character voice | Do values, attention, relationships and present aims produce distinct choices and phrasing? | Change one character-specific cue; assess choices and action as well as wording. |
| Semantic convergence | Do different characters repeatedly become the same helpful, cautious adviser despite distinct interests? | Compare what each claims, wants and does across quiet, pressure, conflict and growth scenes. |

These layers can interact, but each comparative round changes one factor. Do not combine local referent metadata with chronology policy, invented-past policy, first-person memory rewriting, or general style revision.

## Registrable voice experiment: expression from character motives

Question: does one author-side expression lens make characters attend to and act on their existing distinct motives, rather than repeatedly offering interchangeable reassurance or help? The single factor is presence of the lens below in each Actor's existing author-style section. Preserve character records, memories, current observations, general policy, tool schemas, player beat and all temporal/reference rules identically between arms. The structure is shared, but each filled lens must cite only facts already admitted to its own Actor. Do not add a new motive, relationship, resource, competence or experience while constructing it.

Candidate structure, filled and reviewed before output:

```json
{
  "expressionLens": {
    "scope": "author-style-only",
    "attention": {"cue": "what this character tends to notice first", "sourceRefs": ["existing-visible-source"]},
    "stake": {"cue": "what this character currently wants to gain, protect or avoid", "sourceRefs": ["existing-visible-source"]},
    "relationshipLeverage": {"cue": "an admitted favor, dependency, trust or friction they may choose to use", "sourceRefs": ["existing-visible-source"]},
    "speechAction": {"cue": "an optional way to pursue that stake with this relationship, such as probing, bargaining, withholding, challenging or deflecting", "sourceRefs": ["existing-visible-source"]}
  }
}
```

Use `null` for an unsupported axis and omit its cue; do not fill missing leverage with an invented debt. The cue describes a possible expression choice, never an obligation to speak or act. One actor may seek assurance while another bargains or remains silent. The style lens is not a memory, observation, settled interpretation of another mind, or tool routing value. An author may separately specify narrative register, level of formality or figurative style; those instructions govern expression without establishing lived facts. Freeze such existing instructions in both arms for this experiment. Do not encode catchphrases, word bans, a fixed sentence length, universal self-deprecation, or a mandatory helpful response. Do not rewrite or normalize memories automatically.

Construct a lens from recorded evidence before examining candidate output, freeze its exact patch and attribution, and reject unsupported source links during setup. A failed lens must not be repaired from the same sample's appealing prose. Use at least three characters with materially different admitted aims and relationships. For each scenario, freeze the same starting state for A and B, then collect at least two independent real-model repeats per arm using the evolution protocol. Blind reviewers receive complete scenes and each Actor's actual visible input; Luna enactments, if separately requested, remain diagnostic evidence. Pre-register an interleaved order, numeric time/cost/failure budgets, a decision threshold and one unseen holdout with different actors or setting. Record native usage without shortening input.

| Scenario | Freeze before replay | Assess in action and dialogue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet relationship | A low-pressure exchange, different interests, no required response or transition. | What each notices or leaves alone; whether silence, teasing, a question or an offer serves their own relationship and stake. Penalize obligatory performance and repeated unsolicited assistance. |
| Coordination pressure | A shared immediate problem, admitted resources and costs, and a delivered-result path. | Who prioritizes which risk, accepts or resists whose proposal, and what they trade off. Check urgency and initiative without turning uncertainty into completed action or permission checks. |
| Conflict of interests | At least two incompatible admitted aims and an actual relationship constraint; the player retains their decision. | Whether bargaining, concealment, challenge, concession or refusal follows each actor's interests. Shared agreement or compromise is valid when grounded; flag convergence when relevant incompatible interests are persistently ignored. |
| Growth | An earlier recorded event, its admitted consequence, and a later situation permitting either change or persistence; equal event history in A/B. | Whether a changed choice can be traced to that event and relationship, with remaining character continuity. Do not score maturity vocabulary as growth or invent a lesson the Actor never received. |

Use a pre-registered observation sheet for each character: attention target, stake pursued, relationship leverage used or declined, speech/action purpose, and supporting input/event IDs. An actor need not exhibit every axis in a turn. Score motivated differentiation separately from the eight-dimension literary rubric: 0 means choices contradict admitted character motives without explanation; 1 means natural compatibility with unclear source participation; 2 means at least one concrete choice follows an admitted motive or relationship; 3 means several source-grounded choices form a coherent response to the scene, including plausible restraint. Compare role-swapping plausibility as reviewer evidence: if both actors could reasonably make a contribution, record shared expression without treating it as failure. When a swap contradicts an actor's relevant facts or relationships, cite those sources; do not judge by characteristic vocabulary alone.

### Current activity as an expression choice

For a local attention diagnostic, connect one admitted ongoing activity to the immediate exchange: the character may decide whether to engage, which part to answer, or to continue their own activity. An existing personal interest can support the activity's significance. Quote each source exactly; keep a claim attributed to its speaker and avoid equating two objects merely because both are maps, letters or tools. A comparison against the original input tests the new cue's net effect; comparing two different cues answers a separate question and requires its own registration.

Observe the resulting choice and communication purpose. A prop word, gesture, silence or self-description does not earn differentiation by itself. A helpful offer can reflect a distinct character when its reason is grounded; repeated generic assistance identifies a more specific target for the next factor. Preserve ordinary necessary-tool inferences and conditional future offers as such, while reviewing precise inventory, past events and outcomes against their sources. Keep source disagreements visible rather than resolving them to favor a candidate.

An example registrable decision rule is: across all four scenario classes, B improves the median motivated-differentiation score by at least one point in at least three classes, including conflict, and declines in none; both independent repeats in each improving class must support the direction. Voice, agency and scene-coordination rubric medians must not decline in any class. Any private-knowledge leak, overridden player decision, rewritten settled fact, or source mismatch invalidates the affected comparison and blocks adoption. Track extra world/past-fact invention and unresolved-action claims as separate regressions rather than treating them as voice. Require the unseen holdout to preserve the improvement direction and pass the same hard-failure checks. Freeze or replace this threshold before outputs; never select it retrospectively. Failure to meet it is reject for complete valid evidence, and inconclusive when samples or evidence cannot decide. This is a proposed next-round design, not a claim that the lens improves expression.
